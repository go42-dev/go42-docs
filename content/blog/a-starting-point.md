Go42 begins with a practical idea: the context needed to change a service should be available alongside its code.
That includes its development workflow, working conventions, requirements, and significant decisions.

The blueprint provides a starting point. Each application grows its own purpose and operating context from there.

This blog will be a place for development notes, experiments, and explanations of the choices behind Go42.
The [documentation](/docs/) provides the maintained guides for working with the blueprint.
